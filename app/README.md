# Casa Mia

*Make yourself at home, assistant!*

Dashboards that fit your wall tablets, cameras at a glance, and guests welcomed with
a single scan. Casa Mia brings these together in one Home Assistant sidebar panel.

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gazoodle/casa-mia/main/docs/screenshots/home.webp" alt="The Casa Mia panel: the house photo and feature tiles with their current status" width="560">

- **Tablet Layout:** dashboards that fit the screen, with panels that make room as
  cards appear and disappear.
- **Camera Commander:** several cameras composed into one live picture, with a large
  main camera and camera panels around it.
- **Kiosk Satellites:** manage your tablets, keep backups of their settings, open their
  admin pages from anywhere through Home Assistant, and serve updates locally.
- **Guest login:** QR codes that sign visitors straight into their own dashboard.
- **People, phone and SMS:** control who may call or text your home using FONA GSM
  hardware, even when the internet is down.

## Getting started

Choose the features you want in **Configuration**, save, then start the app. Enable
**Show in sidebar** and press **Open web UI** to set them up.

On first start, Casa Mia installs its integration. Restart Home Assistant, then add
**Casa Mia** under **Settings → Devices & services**. The app and integration update
together.

[Read the guides](https://github.com/gazoodle/casa-mia/blob/main/docs/README.md)
for setup, controls and troubleshooting.
