THE TOP OF THE PANEL, AS PROPOSED

The barometer's black hand points at the weather now. Its brass hand, which on a real one is set by its knob to mark an earlier reading, points at tomorrow's. The slip in its window is Home Assistant's own word, because five places on a dial cannot tell snow from rain or fog from cloud. The forecast is a desk calendar with a card for each day. The Outside air meter stays as it is.
