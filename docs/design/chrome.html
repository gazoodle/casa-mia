Chrome

The standard, agreed 2026-10-12 and in use from 2026.10.10-b8. Everything round the content: buttons, dialogs and their buttons, collapsers, menus and selects, our own confirm and ask in place of the browser's, callouts, help text, empty states, switches, sliders and entry boxes. The Now columns are drawn by the live files this page links (app/web/src/ui.module.css for the shared classes, styles.css for the browser's own controls drawn as ours), so this page cannot drift from them; the Before columns are how each looked until then, copied, for the record. The components: ui.tsx (Dialog, Field, Switch, Segmented, Toasts), controls.tsx (Select, Slider), confirm.tsx (confirmBox, askBox); tests/test_no_browser_controls.py fails on a browser dialog or drop-down. Each style has an Id; chrome.html#fold jumps to its row. Light or dark follows your system. Example text is invented. The pills and chips are in pills-and-chips.html.
